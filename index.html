FAQ

Common Questions.

What is Holeshot BMX?

Holeshot BMX is a free BMX racing performance app for iOS and Android. It lets riders, parents, and coaches log race and training times, track improvement over time, manage bike setups, find BMX tracks near them, and compete on leaderboards.

How do I track my BMX race times?

Log section-by-section times for each run — gate start, first straight, corners, and full lap time. Use the built-in stopwatch at the track or enter times manually afterwards. Every run is stored against the track, date, and bike setup used.

Can I find BMX tracks near me?

Yes. Holeshot BMX includes a worldwide directory of hundreds of BMX tracks — across the USA, UK, Australia, New Zealand, and more. Each listing has GPS, surface type, gate height, access info, and social links. If your local track isn't listed yet, you can add it directly in the app.

What BMX gear ratio should I use?

The app's recommendation engine suggests ideal gear ranges and rollout based on your rider's height, age, wheel size, and experience level. It also includes a rollout calculator for precise chainring and sprocket gearing maths.

Does it work with Sqorz timing?

Yes. Holeshot BMX imports your race results directly from Sqorz using your transponder code — automatically matching sessions to the correct track in the app. No manual re-entry needed after a race day.

Is Holeshot BMX free?

Yes, Holeshot BMX is free to download and use on iOS and Android. Sign up above to be notified the moment it launches on your platform.

What BMX frame size do I need?

Holeshot BMX recommends the correct frame class and top tube range based on your rider's height, inseam length, and age. Covers all wheel sizes — 16" micro mini, 18", 20" racing, and 24" cruiser bikes.

Can I manage multiple riders?

Yes. One account supports multiple rider profiles — ideal for parents with more than one child racing, or coaches managing a squad. Each rider has their own sessions, bikes, and performance history.
