Support

Help, data management, and account deletion for the Holeshot BMX app.

Delete your data

You can delete individual records directly within the app at any time. All data belongs to you and can be removed without contacting support.

Data type How to delete
Timing sessions & runs Open the session → tap the menu (⋯) → Delete Session. This removes all run times within it.
Individual run times Inside a session, swipe left on a run → tap Delete.
Rider profiles Settings → Riders → tap the rider → Delete Rider. This also removes all sessions and runs for that rider.
Bike setups Settings → Bike Setups → swipe left on a setup → tap Delete.
Track photos you uploaded Open the track → Photos tab → tap and hold your photo → Remove Photo.
Deleting a rider is permanent — all sessions, run times, and bike setups linked to that rider are also deleted and cannot be recovered.

Delete your account

Deleting your account permanently removes your profile, all riders, sessions, run times, and any other data associated with your account from our servers.

  • Open the Holeshot BMX app and sign in.
  • Tap Settings (bottom-right of the screen).
  • Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  • Confirm the deletion when prompted. Your account and all data will be permanently removed.
This action is irreversible. Once your account is deleted, your data cannot be restored. If you only want to take a break, you can simply log out instead.

Can't access the app?

If you are unable to sign in or access the app, email us directly and we will delete your account and all associated data within 30 days.

Data deletion requests admin@holeshotbmx.com.au Include the email address associated with your account. We respond within 5 business days.
